Trim the Fat

Find the lines that lose money, run more vehicles than they need, or need more. A Line efficiency card in every line window puts the line's balance and its average load side by side, over the same 12 months, and tells you what to do. An overview of all your lines shows the same verdicts in one sortable table.

Features

  • A verdict for each line: Losing money, Too many vehicles, Busy: may need more vehicles or OK. Hover it to see the maths. A line with less than a month of data is Too new to judge, and its money summary is hidden until there's enough to go on.
  • A suggestion of how many vehicles to remove, based on a target load: 50% for single-cargo freight lines (which run empty one way), 70% otherwise.
  • No trim where it would hurt: a line with a busload of passengers waiting at a stop is Busy, a trim never leaves the queues at more than half a busload, and a line whose vehicles are full but deliver little gets a hint to check where the cargo is unloaded.
  • No more vehicles where they'd lose money: a busy line that barely covers its running costs says another vehicle would likely lose money and suggests bigger or newer ones, unless several busloads are waiting.
  • Lines whose income fell, over the last 3 years against the 5 before, are marked Declining ↘, with a hint to check what changed around them.
  • The average load over the last 12 months, next to the load right now.
  • Every vehicle with its age, balance per year and current load, sortable. A vehicle with less than half a year of data shows Too new; between half a year and a year its balance is scaled up to a full year and marked with *.
  • Buy, sell or replace vehicles while the window is open, and the card offers a Vehicles changed: click to refresh button.
  • An overview of every line, opened from a button at the top left of the screen: sortable like the F2 statistics, with verdict and transport filters, a Declining filter, a Hide changed toggle for lines whose vehicles changed in the last 3 months, a search, a summary of what's losing money and what could be trimmed, and an expand button.
  • Translated into all 12 languages of the game.

How to use

  1. Click the button at the top left of the screen (next to the map layers) to see every line, worst first. Filter by verdict or transport type, or search for a line.
  2. Click a line's name to open its window and scroll to the Line efficiency card.
  3. Click a vehicle's name to open its window, where you can sell it. Remove vehicles one at a time: fewer vehicles mean longer waits, which can cost passengers or cargo.

Good to know

  • The base game's Balance is the last 12 months, without the purchase price, and its Utilization is a snapshot of right now. This mod uses the same 12 months for both numbers.
  • The average load counts each passenger or item once per trip, so on lines with many stops it reads lower than seat occupancy. Waiting passengers are checked too, so a crowded line isn't trimmed.
  • A replaced vehicle keeps its history: its balance covers the vehicle's place on the line, not just the new model.
  • This is a cosmetic mod. It doesn't change the simulation, so achievements stay available, and you can add it to or remove it from existing saves.
