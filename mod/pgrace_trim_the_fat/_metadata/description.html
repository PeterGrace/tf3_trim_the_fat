Trim The Fat

Find the lines that lose money or run more vehicles than they need. A Line efficiency card in every line window puts the line's balance and its average load side by side, over the same 12 months, and tells you what to do.

Features

  • A verdict for each line: Losing money, Too many vehicles, Busy: may need more vehicles or OK. Hover it to see the maths. A line with less than a month of data is Too new to judge, and its money summary is hidden until there's enough to go on.
  • A suggestion of how many vehicles to remove, based on a target load: 50% for single-cargo lines (which run empty one way), 70% otherwise.
  • The average load over the last 12 months, next to the load right now.
  • Every vehicle with its age, balance per year and current load, sortable. Vehicles younger than a year are scaled up to a full year and marked with *, so a new vehicle isn't judged on a few months.
  • Buy or sell vehicles while the window is open, and the card offers a Vehicles changed: click to refresh button.
  • Translated into all 12 languages of the game.

How to use

  1. Open a line's window (from the line manager or by clicking a vehicle's line).
  2. Scroll to the Line efficiency card.
  3. Click a vehicle's name to open its window, where you can sell it. Remove vehicles one at a time: fewer vehicles mean longer waits, which can cost passengers or cargo.

Good to know

  • The base game's Balance is the last 12 months, without the purchase price, and its Utilization is a snapshot of right now. This mod uses the same 12 months for both numbers.
  • The average load counts each passenger or item once per trip, so on lines with many stops it reads lower than seat occupancy.
  • This is a cosmetic mod. It doesn't change the simulation, so achievements stay available, and you can add it to or remove it from existing saves.
